Palaces and temples rise beside the river. Beyond the plains, winding paths lead into forested mountains, where a quiet village and monastery lie within reach of the kingdom.

Royal City · River Crossing · Forest Village · Monastery

THE
PROPHECY

In the royal court of Pagan, a prophecy changes the fate of an infant prince. A courageous family carries him into hiding, where his life becomes intertwined with Mg Kan, the general’s grandson.

Beyond the palace, the boys grow up among forest paths, village life, and the lessons of their elders. Friendship becomes brotherhood as the world around them begins to change.
