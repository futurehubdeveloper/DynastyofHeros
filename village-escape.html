Field guide

The road spans 16 × 10 tiles. A level-60 Legend Royal Commander and two soldiers hold the entrance. Three soldiers approach from behind. All five soldiers start at level 45 Elite. Use the open lanes to change formation or flank a guard. Guide all three relatives to a gold entrance tile, or defeat the commander and all five soldiers. An escaped relative leaves the battlefield.

Each relative can move up to three tiles and take one action per round, in either order. Choose a portrait, then a command and a highlighted tile or target. End turn lets the guards respond.

Fists and swords reach one tile. Spears also reach two in a straight line. Weapon skills cost 15% of maximum MP. Two shared herbs each restore up to 35% of the user’s HP.

Family skills. Min Sithu alternates Bal Wike Let Thee and Nyar Wike Let Thee (ဘယ် / ညာ ဝိုက်လက်သီး). Each use delivers one left or right hook for 1.5× damage. His Valor uses his action to give every active relative +10% STR and DEF for five rounds. It costs no MP and is ready again the round it expires. Nga Tint’s Mighty Chop deals 2× damage, with a 20% chance of 5× instead. Nga Pyae Wa’s Triple Thrust delivers up to three spear hits for normal damage each. A surviving target is dazed for its next enemy phase. Royal spear guards can use Double Thrust for two hits when they have enough MP and a single attack would leave the target standing.

Royal Commander. The commander pursues the family with his sword, alternating Cross Slash (two hits) and a regular strike while MP lasts. He prioritizes Battle Frenzy and attacks before using potions. His first Battle Frenzy has a 50% chance on turns 1 and 2 and is guaranteed from turn 3 as soon as he can act and boost a soldier. Later eligible rounds have a 50% chance. After two missed rolls he uses Battle Frenzy on the next eligible turn. Each cast permanently adds up to 10 levels to each surviving soldier, capped at the commander’s level of 60, and starts a five-turn cooldown. The commander also carries three potions. Each heals a living member of his side for up to 100 HP. Casting or healing uses his action.

Physical damage = STR × STR ÷ (STR + target DEF), rounded down with a minimum of 1. Keyboard users can focus the board, move the tile cursor with the arrow keys, and press Enter.
