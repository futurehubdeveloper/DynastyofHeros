Field guide

The road spans 16 × 10 tiles. Three guards hold the approach to the gate while three close in from behind. Use the open lanes to change formation or flank a guard. Guide all three relatives to a gold gate tile, or defeat all six royal guards. An escaped relative leaves the battlefield.

Each relative can move up to three tiles and take one action per round, in either order. Choose a portrait, then a command and a highlighted tile or target. End turn lets the guards respond.

Fists and swords reach one tile. Spears also reach two in a straight line. Skills cost 15% of maximum MP and deal 1.5× damage. Two shared herbs each restore up to 35% of the user’s HP.

Physical damage = STR × STR ÷ (STR + target DEF), rounded down with a minimum of 1. Keyboard users can focus the board, move the tile cursor with the arrow keys, and press Enter.
